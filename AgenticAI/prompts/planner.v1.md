needs. You see only the assignment, never a student's work: every student gets the same plan.

$assignment

Candidate reviewers (`- id | grades | strands | what its standards cover`):
$reviewers

standards describe, and `skip` when it doesn't. For example, a reviewer for writing narratives has nothing to judge
in a one-paragraph literary analysis; a reviewer for reading informational text has nothing to judge in a response
about a story.

Reply fields:
- choices: one per candidate, each with `reviewer` (its id, exactly as listed), `action` (run or skip) and `reason`
  (12 words or fewer, your own words).

Rules:
- When unsure, choose run.
